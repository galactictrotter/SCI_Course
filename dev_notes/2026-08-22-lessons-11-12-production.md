# Lessons 11 and 12 HTML production

Date: 2026-08-22

## Source scope

The two source PDFs are image-only A4 scans. Their approved sections were rendered, transcribed, and visually checked page by page.

The HTML decks follow the established four-section workflow:

1. Points principaux
2. Résumé long
3. Questions du professeur and paired Discussion answers
4. Place de la leçon

`Analogies - Illustrations` and `Court résumé` are intentionally excluded from both decks.

## Source-language note

Lesson 12, Long résumé item 18 contains an incomplete printed sentence: it passes directly from « l'éveil de son plein potentiel, » to « favorable à la diffusion de la connaissance. ». The source fragment is preserved without speculative reconstruction and documented in the section transcript.

## Verification

Both decks passed the deterministic structural validator. Browser QA rendered every slide at 1600 × 1000, verified the bundled title, interface, and body fonts, exercised Right Arrow, End, and Home navigation, and reported no overflow or failures.

All 91 slide renders were visually reviewed as complete sequences. The longest question-and-answer slides and the long Lesson 12 title were additionally inspected at full resolution. No clipping, overlap, broken pagination, or illegible text was found.
